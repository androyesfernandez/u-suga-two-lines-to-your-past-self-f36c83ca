## The entry

```text
Don't send that furious resignation text to your boss from the parking lot outside the warehouse on your lunch break on August 9th.
He never answered, payroll cut you off that Friday, and you lost the health cover your daughter needed the very next month.
```

## Why this line won

Six drafts from two rival drafters were duelled round-robin under the kit judge's five criteria, and a rival critic adjudicated the final head-to-head. This line — the parking-lot resignation text — won because its second line is a chain of real, external, same-week consequences: the boss never answers, payroll cuts you off that Friday, and your daughter loses the health cover she needed the very next month. The runner-up, a last call to Grandma, is more moving, but its second line lands on a feeling and a mental habit rather than a changed outcome, and it spends its reveal in the first line. The critic's one tweak — "angry two-line" became "furious" — removed a wink at the entry's own form. The full tournament record is in [duel-test.md](duel-test.md).
